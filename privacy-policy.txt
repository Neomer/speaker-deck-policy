Speaker Deck for Android - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Speaker Deck app for Android ("the app") handles and why.


1. Your decks and cards

Everything you create in the app - deck titles, card text, talk and card times, settings - is stored only on your device. The developer has no server and never receives this content.

The app does not require registration and does not show ads.

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings; the developer has no access to it.

If you use the backup feature (Premium), the app writes your decks to a file in a location you choose. The app does not upload this file anywhere; where it goes after that is up to you.


2. Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, for example how often a presentation or a rehearsal is opened. Firebase may collect:

- app usage events (app opens, sessions, feature usage, in-app purchase events);
- device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, language);
- identifiers: the Firebase app instance ID and, if allowed by your device settings, the Android advertising ID;
- approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level).

The events the app sends itself contain only a technical deck number. Deck titles and card text are never sent.

This data is used only for analytics. It is not sold and is not used by the developer for advertising. It is processed by Google as a service provider. How Google handles this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).


Crash reports

If the app crashes or stops responding, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so the developer can find and fix the problem. A report contains the technical details of the error (the place in the app's code where it happened and the app's state at that moment), device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk space, orientation) and a random Crashlytics installation ID. It may also include the list of recent analytics events described above. Crash reports never contain deck titles or card text. They are processed by Google as a service provider and used only to fix errors.

3. Purchases

Premium is available as a one-time purchase or as a monthly subscription through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the developer never sees your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether Premium is active.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play app (Payments and subscriptions).


4. Ratings

The app may occasionally show the standard Google Play rating window. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play; the app does not receive them and does not know whether you left one.


5. Permissions

- Internet - for analytics, crash reports and checking purchases with Google Play.
- Vibrate - for the vibration on card change and the time signals.
- Billing - for Premium purchases.
- Advertising ID - used only by Firebase Analytics, as described above.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.


6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from them.


7. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data (Settings > Apps > Speaker Deck > Storage) removes all your decks and cards from the device.

Analytics data and crash reports are kept by Google for a limited period and then deleted automatically. To ask a question or request deletion of analytics data or crash reports related to your device, contact the developer at the address below.
