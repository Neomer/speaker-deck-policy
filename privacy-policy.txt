1. General Information

This application does not collect, store, or process any personal data from users.

The app operates entirely offline. All data created within the app is stored locally on the user’s device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

2. Data Collection

The application:

Does not require registration

Does not collect personal information

Does not collect device identifiers

Does not access location, camera, microphone, or storage

Does not use analytics services

Does not use advertising SDKs

Does not transmit any data to third parties

3. Data Storage

All user-generated data remains solely on the user’s device.
The developer does not have access to this data.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate with third-party services that collect user data.

5. Children’s Privacy

This application is intended for users aged 13 and older.
It is not directed to children under the age of 13.

The developer does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

6. User Control

Since all data is stored locally on the device:

Users may delete app data at any time via device settings.

Uninstalling the application removes all stored data.
