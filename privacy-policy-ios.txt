Speaker Deck for iPhone - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Speaker Deck app for iPhone ("the app") handles and why.


1. Your decks and cards

Everything you create in the app - deck titles, card text, talk and card times, settings - is stored only on your device. The developer has no server and never receives this content. The app does not sync your decks through iCloud.

The app does not require registration and does not show ads.

If iCloud Backup or computer backup is turned on, iOS may include the app's data in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by Apple under your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it.

If you use the backup feature (Premium), the app saves your decks to a file in a place you choose (for example iCloud Drive, Files or AirDrop). The app does not upload this file anywhere; where it goes after that is up to you.


2. Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, for example how often a presentation or a rehearsal is opened. Firebase may collect:

- app usage events (app opens, sessions, feature usage, in-app purchase events);
- device and app information (device model, iOS version, app version, language);
- identifiers: the Firebase app instance ID and the identifier for vendor (IDFV), which is unique to this developer's apps on your device;
- approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level).

The app does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. For this reason it never asks for tracking permission.

The events the app sends itself contain only a technical deck number. Deck titles and card text are never sent.

This data is used only for analytics. It is not sold and is not used for advertising. It is processed by Google as a service provider. How Google handles this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites


3. Purchases

Premium is available as a one-time purchase or as a monthly subscription through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never sees your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the App Store of whether Premium is active.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.


4. Ratings

The app may occasionally show the standard App Store rating window. Your rating and review go directly to Apple; the app does not receive them and does not know whether you left one.


5. Device access

The app uses vibration (haptics) for card changes and time signals. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.


6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from them.


7. Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all your decks and cards from the device.

Analytics data is kept by Google for a limited period and then deleted automatically. To ask a question or request deletion of analytics data related to your device, contact the developer at the address below.
